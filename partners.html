What is the recommended first pilot?

The recommended first pilot is K-Culture Motion Lab: a four-week online module for design students and motion creators that produces a 30-second spatial loop and spatial mockup.

Who can partner with VARIS?

VARIS works with platforms, academies, universities, corporations, studios, agencies, and industry associations.

How long is the pilot?

The focused pilot runs for four weeks: align, launch, review learner work and critique, then decide the next scope.

What does a typical pilot include?

A typical pilot includes up to 20 learners, two group critiques, and one final review.
