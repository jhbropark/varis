## About VARIS

VARIS is a Korea-based education brand operated by SIA.HAUS, a creative studio specializing in media art, immersive experiences, and digital content production. Our courses draw from real production workflows, helping learners develop skills through project-based assignments and expert feedback.

**Production Background:**
- K-pop artist visuals (SM Entertainment, etc.)
- Public media art installations (Incheon Airport, National Heritage projects)
- Immersive spatial experiences and exhibitions

## Partnership Models

### 1. VOD Distribution
- **Unit:** Complete project course with localized subtitles
- **Revenue:** Per-sale or subscription revenue share

### 2. Co-Branded Cohort
- **Unit:** VOD + assignments + scheduled expert critiques
- **Revenue:** Per-cohort fee or revenue share
- **Best For:** Creator academies, bootcamps, education institutions

### 3. Institutional License
- **Unit:** Seats + duration based content and instructor materials
- **Revenue:** Annual or per-term licensing fee
- **Best For:** Universities, corporate training departments

## Pilot Program

We recommend starting with a small pilot to validate fit before scaling.

### Typical Pilot Scope
- **Partner:** 1
- **Module:** 1 (K-Culture Motion Lab recommended)
- **Learners:** Up to 20
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Expert Sessions:** 2 group critiques + 1 final review

### Success Metrics
- Paid enrollment count
- Course start rate
- Assignment submission rate
- Quality assessment pass rate
- Learner satisfaction score
- Refund rate and reasons
- Support time per learner
- Contribution margin after localization costs
